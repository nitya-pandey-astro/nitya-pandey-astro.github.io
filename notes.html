Poetry · Nitya Pandey

What Does a Smile Do?

Before a presentation,
when my hands begin to tremble
and my thoughts run faster than my voice,
I smile.
Not because I am fearless,
but because the smile reminds me
that I have made it this far
and that I can still begin.

The same smile follows me
when the room is empty
and I am carrying pain
I do not know how to explain.
It does not make the pain disappear,
it simply makes it
a little easier to carry.

Then it reaches beyond me.
It becomes a small bridge
between my heart
and the people around me,
a quiet way of saying,
“I am here.”

And when the day feels too heavy,
when I am tired of trying
and tired of doubting myself,
a smile gives me back
a little warmth,
a little courage,
a reason to keep going.

So what does a smile do?
Sometimes it calms me.
Sometimes it connects me.
Sometimes it helps me hold
what hurts.
But most of all,
it reminds me
that even on difficult days,
I am still here.
